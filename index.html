Areas of Focus

Practical tools. A relaxed place to figure things out.

Life Transitions

Whether you're heading to college, figuring out adulthood, changing careers, or adjusting to a new chapter, big transitions can bring a mix of excitement, uncertainty, and self-doubt. We'll work together to help you feel more grounded, make decisions with confidence, and move forward without feeling like you have to have everything figured out.

Anxiety & Stress

Anxiety is exhausting. If your brain is always planning for the worst, replaying conversations, or convincing you everything is urgent, we'll work together to understand what's happening and build practical tools that help in everyday life, not just in therapy.

Neurodivergence

This is a space where you don't have to explain or apologize for how your brain works. Together, we'll better understand your thinking style, reduce overwhelm, and develop practical strategies that support your strengths while making day-to-day challenges feel more manageable.

Perfectionism

Perfectionism has a funny way of convincing us that if we just try a little harder, worry a little more, or get everything exactly right, we'll finally feel okay. Instead, it usually leaves us exhausted. We'll work on breaking that cycle so you can feel confident without carrying the weight of impossible expectations.

Common Questions

A few things people usually ask.

What's a first session actually like?

We'll spend most of it just talking. I'll ask about what brought you in, what's felt hard lately, and what you're hoping is different a few months from now. There's no clipboard or script, just a real conversation.

How does virtual therapy work?

Sessions happen over a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform. You'll get a link before each appointment, log on from wherever you're comfortable, anywhere in Texas, and we'll meet face-to-face on screen.

What are the benefits of virtual therapy?

Virtual therapy fits into real life. No commute, no waiting room, no rearranging your whole afternoon, just log on from home, your car during a lunch break, or wherever you have privacy and decent Wi-Fi (and let's be honest, nobody misses sitting in traffic anyway). You're also not limited to therapists within driving distance, so you can find the right fit rather than just the closest one, and most people find they open up just as easily in a familiar space as they would in an office.

Do you accept insurance?

I'm in-network with Aetna and United Healthcare, and I also offer self-pay options. You can note your plan when you reach out through the contact form.

What areas of Texas do you work with?

Since sessions are entirely virtual, I work with older teens and adults anywhere in Texas. I'm based in Austin, but your zip code doesn't matter as long as you're in the state.

I've never done therapy before. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Plenty of people I work with are brand new to therapy. We'll go at your pace, and you're welcome to ask as many questions as you'd like along the way.
